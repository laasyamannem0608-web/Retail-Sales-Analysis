# Retail Sales Analysis using Python

## Project Overview
This project analyses a retail sales dataset using Python to identify sales trends, profitability, customer purchasing patterns, and regional performance. The analysis includes data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), data visualisation, and business recommendations to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## Objectives
- Analyse retail sales data.
- Identify the most profitable categories and regions.
- Compare sales and profit across different categories and sub-categories.
- Understand customer purchasing patterns.
- Generate business insights and recommendations.

---

## Technologies Used
- Python
- NumPy
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- VS Code

## Business Insights
1. Technology is the most profitable product category.
2. The West region generates the highest sales and profit.
3. Phones are the best-selling sub-category.
4. Chairs are the highest-selling non-technology sub-category.
5. Most sales transactions fall between 0 and 5,000, with only a few high-value transactions.
6. Sales and Profit have a positive correlation, indicating that higher sales generally lead to higher profit.
7. Office Supplies has the highest number of customer transactions.

---

## Business Recommendations
1. Expand the Technology category to maximise profit.
2. Improve the Furniture category by reviewing pricing, discounts, and marketing strategies.
3. Expand business operations in the West and East regions.
4. Increase the inventory and promotion of high-performing products such as Phones and Chairs.
5. Focus on improving profitability in the Office Supplies category since it has the largest customer base.
6. Maintain healthy profit margins by increasing sales while controlling discounts and operational costs.
